Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

What age group is the Play Group program for?
Our Play Group is designed specifically for children aged 2 to 3 years. This is a crucial developmental window and our program is carefully tailored to match their cognitive, social, and physical milestones.
What are the program timings?
Classes run Monday through Friday from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM. This half-day schedule is ideal for young children, giving them quality learning time without overexertion. Extended care options may also be available — contact us to find out more.
Is the curriculum play-based or academic?
Both! Our proprietary Seven Petal Approach is rooted in scientifically backed play-based learning. Children develop essential cognitive, language, motor, and social-emotional skills through structured play — so they learn deeply while having the time of their lives.
How do I enrol my child?
Simply click "Enroll Now" below, fill in the short form, and our admissions team will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a visit and walk you through the next steps. Seats are limited each term, so early registration is encouraged!
What safety measures are in place?
Child safety is our absolute top priority. We maintain CCTV monitoring throughout the premises, secure gated entry, trained staff with first-aid certification, strict drop-off & pick-up protocols, allergen-aware snack policies, and regularly sanitised learning spaces.
