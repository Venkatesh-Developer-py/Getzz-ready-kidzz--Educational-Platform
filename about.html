Our Story

Born from a Mother's Dream

Getzz Ready Kidzz was founded in 2014 by a group of passionate educators and parents who felt Coimbatore deserved a school that truly put children first. Frustrated with one-size-fits-all preschools, our founder envisioned a place where every child's curiosity is honoured, every milestone celebrated, and every family feels like an extension of the school community.


What began as a small playgroup of 12 children in a sunlit home has grown into Coimbatore's most trusted Montessori-inspired school and day care — serving over 500 families across the city, with award-winning programs from infant care all the way to kindergarten.

What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision
& Promise to You

Everything we do — from our curriculum to the colour of our walls — flows from three guiding principles.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inclusive, and stimulating environment where every child between 6 months and 6 years can explore their potential with joy, confidence, and a lifelong love of learning.

🌅

Our Vision

To be the most trusted early childhood education partner in Coimbatore — shaping curious, compassionate, and capable young citizens who are ready for the world.

🤝

Our Promise

Every child who walks through our doors will be seen, heard, and celebrated. We promise to communicate openly with every parent and treat your child exactly as we would our own.
